Our mission

We study how energy transfer and atomic transport across scales govern structural and chemical transformations during materials synthesis. Combining advanced in situ diagnostics, computation and data science, we seek to understand and predict nonequilibrium synthesis pathways, from precursor conversion and intermediate formation to the emergence of target materials.

Building on this foundation, we aim to develop world models for materials synthesis that infer evolving material states from observations and predict how different processing actions shape future reaction pathways. Grounded in physical constraints and refined through experimental feedback, these models would guide the precise synthesis of materials with targeted compositions, structures and phases.

Our approach

Observe transformations. Predict pathways. Guide synthesis.
