Our vision

Build the materials we need.

We envision a future in which materials with the properties society needs can be designed, reliably synthesized and validated in use.

THE BIG PICTURE

From material design to experimental reality.

02 · THE SYNTHESIZABILITY GAP

From a promising candidate to a reliable synthesis route.

Which precursors, pathways and conditions will reliably produce the material state we need?

Many established materials have workable recipes. For new targets or conditions, reliable synthesis requires more than thermodynamic stability: intermediates, competing reactions, kinetics and transport shape what forms.

03 · OUR POSITION

AI for predictive synthesis science.

We connect experimental observations, physical principles and AI to predict how materials form.

OUR MISSION

Understand formation. Guide synthesis.

Develop experimentally grounded models of material formation to predict competing pathways and guide synthesis toward desired materials.

OUR RESEARCH STRATEGY

Observe. Model. Test and guide.

  1. Observe formationInfer evolving material states from synthesis records and time-resolved, multimodal measurements.
  2. Model and predictCombine AI with thermodynamics, kinetics and transport to predict evolving states and competing pathways.
  3. Test and guideTest predictions, distinguish competing explanations and choose the next route or processing conditions.

What progress means: predictive accuracy under new conditions, reproducible target formation, and experimentally verified function.

One core question. Three connected directions.
