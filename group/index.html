Our mission

We bring artificial intelligence together with synthesis science to understand how materials form and to develop a quantitative basis for predicting and controlling their synthesis. Our research examines how thermodynamic driving forces and reaction kinetics, coupled with energy transfer and atomic transport across scales, govern structural and chemical transformations, from precursor conversion and intermediate formation to the emergence of target materials.

We combine advanced in situ and ex situ characterization, physics-based computation and data science to investigate reaction mechanisms and predict nonequilibrium synthesis pathways. Systematically designed experiments allow us to test competing mechanistic hypotheses and establish how processing conditions determine phase formation. We are developing machine-learning and autonomous experimentation methods to select informative experiments and refine predictions through experimental feedback. Our goal is to guide the precise synthesis of materials with targeted compositions, structures and phases.

Our approach

Observe transformations. Test mechanisms. Predict synthesis.
