OUR MISSION

Understand formation.
Guide synthesis.

We combine AI, physical mechanisms and experimental feedback to understand how materials form and guide their reliable synthesis.

Our focus is the path from precursors through evolving intermediates to target and competing phases. We develop models that connect processing conditions to these pathways, test their predictions through characterization and experiments, and use the evidence to refine the next synthesis decision. The goal is to make materials with the intended structure and experimentally verified function.

THE QUESTION THAT CONNECTS OUR WORK

How do we turn a promising design into a material that works?

  1. Define the needApplication requirements and target properties
  2. Find a realizable pathCandidate structures, precursors and formation pathways
  3. Make it. Test it.Controlled synthesis and functional validation

Experimental evidence feeds back into material targets, synthesis choices and predictive models.

THE SYNTHESIS GAP

A candidate structure is the beginning.

Computational design can suggest promising materials. Making a chosen target requires more: suitable precursors, selective reaction pathways and conditions that favor its formation. Thermodynamic stability alone does not establish synthesizability; the properties of the material we actually make must then be tested.
