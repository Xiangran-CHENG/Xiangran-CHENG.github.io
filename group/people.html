GROUP MENTOR

Xiangran Cheng

Research Scientist
Beijing Zhongguancun Academy

Xiangran studies how materials form and how synthesis shapes their structure and properties. Her research combines physical modeling, AI and experiments to guide the design of energy materials, alongside ongoing work on scientific agents and laboratory automation.

She joined Beijing Zhongguancun Academy in 2025, building on research in polymer electrolytes, battery interfaces and flexible batteries. Her academic background includes studies at Dalian University of Technology and doctoral research at Fudan University. She also founded a materials startup and gained battery manufacturing experience at Tesla’s Shanghai Gigafactory.
