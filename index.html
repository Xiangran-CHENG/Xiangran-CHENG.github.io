I study how materials form, and how AI can help us make them.我研究材料如何形成，以及 AI 如何帮助我们合成材料。

Email邮箱 Google Scholar GitHub CV Portfolio & Gallery作品与图库

I am a Research Scientist at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, working at the intersection of materials science, AI and automation. My research connects reaction pathways and synthesizability with polymer electrolytes and battery interfaces.我是北京中关村学院研究科学家，研究材料科学、AI 与自动化的交叉问题。我关注反应路径与材料可合成性，以及聚合物电解质和电池界面。

A question that drives my work: can we predict how a synthesis reaction will unfold, and use that prediction to choose the next experiment? In the Cheng Research Group, we are exploring world models for materials synthesis and working toward autonomous laboratories that learn from experimental feedback.我最关心的问题是：能否预测合成反应如何演化，并据此选择下一步实验？在 Cheng Research Group，我们正在探索材料合成的世界模型，并致力于建设能够从实验反馈中学习的自主实验室。

Research研究

Four connected directions, from materials formation to autonomous research.四条相互连接的主线，从材料形成走向自主研究。

  1. 01

    Materials Synthesizability and Reaction Mechanisms材料可合成性与反应机制

    We study how thermodynamics, kinetics and interfacial reactions govern the path from precursors through intermediates to target materials. Literature mining, AI prediction and process characterization help us identify competing pathways and design experiments that distinguish between mechanistic explanations.研究热力学、动力学与界面反应如何共同决定从前驱体、经中间态到目标材料的形成路径。结合文献挖掘、AI 预测与过程表征，识别竞争路径，并设计能够区分不同机制解释的实验。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  2. 02

    Battery Materials and Interfaces电池材料与界面

    Our work connects polymer and solid electrolytes, cathode materials and electrode–electrolyte interfaces. We combine computation, high-throughput screening, synthesis validation and in-situ characterization to relate material structure and transport to stability and performance.围绕聚合物与固态电解质、正极材料以及电极—电解质界面，结合计算、高通量筛选、合成验证和原位表征，建立材料结构、传输行为与稳定性、性能之间的联系。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  3. 03

    Multiphysics Modeling and Spatiotemporal Prediction多物理场建模与时空预测

    We develop models of coupled physical fields, spatial structures and time-dependent signals across materials, devices and processing equipment. Physics-based simulation and data-driven prediction connect observed behavior with forecasts that can be tested under changing conditions.面向材料、器件与工艺设备中的耦合物理场、空间结构和时变信号，结合物理仿真与数据驱动预测，建立能够在变化条件下检验的状态预测方法。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  4. 04

    Autonomous Laboratories and Scientific Research自主实验室与自主科学研究

    We are working toward systems that connect laboratory execution, experimental decision-making and scientific reasoning. The goal is to make experiments useful for both improving materials and refining explanations, with researchers able to inspect the evidence behind each decision.建设连接实验执行、实验决策与科学推理的研究系统，使实验既能服务于材料优化，也能用于完善机制解释，并让研究人员能够检查每项决策背后的证据。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
