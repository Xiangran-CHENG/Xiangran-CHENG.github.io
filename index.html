研究主线Research Agenda

我最关心的问题，是反应路径如何决定材料是否真正“可合成”。 A central question in my work is how reaction pathways determine whether a material is truly synthesizable.

核心科学问题Core Question

当前很多材料可以被理论设计出来，却难以在实验中稳定制备。我尤其关注硫化物固态电解质中 “可设计”到“可制备”之间的落差，也就是 materials synthesizability gap。 Many materials can be computationally proposed, but cannot be reliably synthesized in experiment. I am particularly interested in the synthesizability gap in sulfide solid electrolytes and other complex inorganic systems.

研究方法Approach

我结合文献工艺先验挖掘、原位 XRD/Raman、多模态时序数据分析和机器学习建模， 去描述“前驱体—工艺条件—中间相演化—最终物相”之间的定量关系。 I combine literature mining, in-situ XRD/Raman, multimodal time-series analysis, and machine learning to quantify the relationship between precursors, process conditions, intermediate-phase evolution, and final phases.

长期目标Long-term Goal

我希望推动材料设计判据从“终点态判断”转向“路径感知判断”，并最终构建 AI-native autonomous materials lab，让材料发现、合成与验证形成真正的闭环。 My long-term goal is to shift materials design from end-state judgment to path-aware judgment, and ultimately build an AI-native autonomous materials lab for closed-loop discovery, synthesis, and validation.

当前项目Current Projects

围绕硫化物固态电解质与自主实验展开的几个方向。 Selected directions in sulfide solid electrolytes and autonomous experimentation.

路径感知可合成性Path-aware Synthesizability

构建包含成功与失败样本的反应路径数据集，提取中间相出现顺序、持续区间、 消失速率与竞争相演化等描述符，建立可解释的可合成性预测模型。 Building reaction-pathway datasets with both success and failure cases, and extracting descriptors such as intermediate-phase order, duration, disappearance rate, and competing-phase evolution for interpretable synthesizability prediction.

原位多模态动力学表征In-situ Multimodal Kinetics

通过原位 XRD 和原位 Raman 连续跟踪固相反应过程，理解前驱体分解、 中间相竞争与目标相生长之间的动力学联系。 Using in-situ XRD and Raman to continuously monitor solid-state reactions and understand the kinetic relationships among precursor decomposition, competing intermediates, and target-phase growth.
