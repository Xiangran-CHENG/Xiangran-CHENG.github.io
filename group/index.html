关于我们About Us

Materials Builders

我们关注材料在真实合成过程中如何形成，探索连接材料科学、人工智能与实验验证的研究方法。We study how materials form during real synthesis processes, bringing together materials science, artificial intelligence, and experimental validation.

从前驱体转化、中间相竞争到目标相生长，我们希望建立能够识别反应状态、预测工艺干预后演变，并接受实验检验的材料合成世界模型。From precursor conversion and competing intermediates to target-phase growth, we aim to build world models that infer reaction states, predict the effects of process interventions, and can be tested through experiments.

研究方向Research

沿着反应路径，理解材料如何形成。Follow the reaction pathway. Understand how materials form.

01

反应状态识别Observe the reaction

结合原位 XRD、Raman 与多模态时序分析，追踪前驱体转化、中间相竞争和目标相生长，寻找影响后续反应的关键状态。Use in-situ XRD, Raman, and multimodal time-series analysis to follow precursor conversion, competing intermediates, and target-phase growth.

原位表征 / 多模态数据 / 中间相In-situ probes / Multimodal data / Intermediates
02

合成路径推演Model what happens next

结合反应数据与热力学、动力学约束，探索工艺操作如何改变相演变与竞争路径，建立可接受实验检验的预测模型。Combine reaction data with thermodynamic and kinetic constraints to explore how process choices change phase evolution and competing pathways.

世界模型 / 物理约束 / 可合成性World models / Physical constraints / Synthesizability
03

实验闭环验证Put predictions to the test

面向自动化实验与主动学习，比较候选合成方案。用新的实验观测区分竞争解释，更新反应模型与下一步决策。Connect automated experiments with active learning. Compare candidate synthesis routes, test competing explanations, and update the model and the next experiment.

主动学习 / 自动化实验 / 预测检验Active learning / Automation / Validation

导师GROUP MENTOR

程翔然 Xiangran Cheng

北京中关村学院 · 研究科学家Research Scientist · Beijing Zhongguancun Academy

研究位于材料科学、人工智能与自动化的交叉点，关注硫化物固态电解质、聚合物电解质与界面电化学，探索连接文献先验、原位多模态表征、路径感知预测与闭环实验验证的材料可合成性研究框架。Working at the intersection of materials science, AI, and automation, with interests in sulfide solid electrolytes, polymer electrolytes, and interfacial electrochemistry. His research explores path-aware synthesizability through literature priors, in-situ multimodal characterization, predictive modeling, and experimental validation.
