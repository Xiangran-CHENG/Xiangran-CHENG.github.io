I am a Research Scientist at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, working at the intersection of materials science, AI and automation. My research connects reaction pathways and synthesizability with polymer electrolytes and battery interfaces.我是北京中关村学院研究科学家，研究材料科学、AI 与自动化的交叉问题。我关注反应路径与材料可合成性，以及聚合物电解质和电池界面。

A question that drives my work: can we predict how a synthesis reaction will unfold, and use that prediction to choose the next experiment? In the Cheng Research Group, we are exploring world models for materials synthesis and working toward autonomous laboratories that learn from experimental feedback.我最关心的问题是：能否预测合成反应如何演化，并据此选择下一步实验？在 Cheng Research Group，我们正在探索材料合成的世界模型，并致力于建设能够从实验反馈中学习的自主实验室。

Research研究

From understanding the path of a reaction to building tools that help us steer it.从理解反应路径，到构建能够指导合成的工具。

World models for materials synthesis材料合成的世界模型

We aim to learn how precursors, processing conditions and intermediate phases shape reaction outcomes. Time-resolved observations and models can help us test competing pathways and choose informative experiments.我们希望理解前驱体、工艺条件与中间相如何共同决定反应结果，通过时间分辨观测与模型检验竞争路径，并选择更有信息量的实验。

Electrolytes and battery interfaces电解质与电池界面

My work on polymer gel electrolytes and fiber batteries asks how molecular design, ion transport and interfacial stability influence device performance. These questions also motivate my interest in solid-state electrolytes.我在聚合物凝胶电解质与纤维电池方面的研究，关注分子设计、离子传输和界面稳定性如何影响器件性能。这些问题也推动我进一步研究固态电解质。

Autonomous materials laboratories自主材料实验室

Our long-term goal is to connect synthesis planning, robotic experiments, characterization and model updates in a closed loop. The focus is on learning from experimental outcomes, including unsuccessful reactions.我们的长期目标是将合成规划、机器人实验、表征与模型更新连接成闭环，从实验结果中学习，也包括未成功的反应。
