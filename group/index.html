AI FOR MATERIALS SYNTHESIS

Our vision

Make materials synthesis predictable.

We envision a future in which understanding how materials form helps us anticipate competing phases and choose conditions that reliably produce the intended material.

Our mission ↓ Explore our research ↗

OUR MISSION

Understand formation.
Guide synthesis.

We develop physically grounded AI models that connect precursors and processing conditions to evolving material states and reaction pathways.

By combining in situ and ex situ characterization, computation and targeted experiments, we aim to explain phase selection, predict how synthesis changes with conditions, and identify informative next experiments. Our long-term goal is to turn this understanding into reliable control over material composition, structure and phase.

THE QUESTION THAT CONNECTS OUR WORK

Which synthesis conditions lead to which material—and why?

  1. Precursors & conditionsWhat we start with and control
  2. Evolving reaction pathwaysIntermediates, interfaces and transport
  3. Target & competing phasesWhat forms, when and why

Our scientific focus

Three commitments to the same question
01

Follow materials as they form.

Track how precursors transform into intermediates, target phases and competing products. We focus on how reactions at solid–solid and solid–liquid interfaces shape the path to a target material.

Reaction mechanisms ↗
02

Connect AI with reaction physics.

Use thermodynamic driving forces, kinetics and transport across scales to inform AI models, constrain predictions and test explanations for why particular pathways emerge.

Physical modeling ↗
03

Make predictions testable.

Design experiments that distinguish competing explanations and test predictions under changed synthesis conditions. We are developing autonomous workflows to refine models through experimental feedback.

Experimental feedback ↗
