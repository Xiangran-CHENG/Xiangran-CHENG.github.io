Materials development connects how a material is made with the structure it forms, the properties it exhibits and the function it delivers.

02 · THE SYNTHESIZABILITY GAP

From a promising candidate to a reliable synthesis route.

Which precursors, pathways and conditions will reliably produce the material state we need?

Many established materials already have workable recipes. The gap arises when we cannot yet predict how a target will form under new routes or conditions. Thermodynamic stability alone does not determine the outcome: intermediates, competing reactions, kinetics and transport shape what is actually made.

03 · OUR POSITION

AI for predictive synthesis science.

We focus on material formation: connecting experimental observations, physical principles and AI to understand how precursors evolve and to predict the pathways that lead to desired materials.

How we approach this challenge ↓

OUR MISSION

Understand formation. Guide synthesis.

Develop experimentally grounded models of material formation to predict competing pathways and guide synthesis toward desired materials.

Given precursors and processing conditions, we study which intermediates emerge, which phases compete and how the material state evolves. We test these predictions and use the results to refine both our models and our synthesis decisions.

OUR RESEARCH STRATEGY

Observe. Model. Test and guide.

  1. Observe formationInfer evolving material states from synthesis records and time-resolved, multimodal measurements.
  2. Model and predictCombine AI with thermodynamics, kinetics and transport to predict evolving states and competing pathways.
  3. Test and guideTest predictions, distinguish competing explanations and choose the next route or processing conditions.

What progress means: predictive accuracy under new conditions, reproducible target formation, and experimentally verified function.
