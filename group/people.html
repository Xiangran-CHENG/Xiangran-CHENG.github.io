OUR SHARED CULTURE

How we work together

Curiosity, courage, resilience and empathy guide how we do science. These are commitments we make to one another, in both the questions we pursue and the way we pursue them.

  • Cross boundaries

    We connect materials science, condensed matter physics, computer vision and robotics. We learn from one another and follow useful ideas beyond the boundaries of our own disciplines.

  • Be curious and courageous

    We question assumptions and try ideas whose outcomes are uncertain. When research gets difficult, we stay resilient, adapt our approach and keep asking what we can learn.

  • Fail fast, learn honestly

    We turn ideas into focused tests, share what does not work and revise our approach. Each attempt should help us ask a better question or design a better experiment.

  • Talk is cheap. Show me data.

    We test claims against observations, make uncertainty visible and welcome results that challenge our expectations. Careful measurements and reproducible analysis give us grounds to change our minds.

  • Collaborate with empathy

    We listen across differences, share knowledge and credit, and support one another through setbacks. Scientific progress and the growth of our teammates are responsibilities we share.

  • Think big. Build to last.

    We pair idealism with practical steps: ambitious questions, realistic milestones and work that matters. We value lasting understanding and useful results over quick wins.

From pictures to understanding

Together, we aim to build a laboratory where automation and intelligent tools help us test ideas quickly, learn from setbacks and iterate. We want to turn ideas we can picture into understanding we can test, and understanding into the next experiment.

GROUP MENTOR

Xiangran Cheng

Research Scientist
Beijing Zhongguancun Academy

Xiangran's research spans polymer electrolytes, electrode–electrolyte interfaces, flexible batteries and manufacturing. Since joining Beijing Zhongguancun Academy in 2025, Xiangran has focused on bringing materials science, AI and automation together to develop autonomous laboratories.

Xiangran studied at Dalian University of Technology, took part in an exchange at the National University of Singapore in 2018, and began doctoral research at Fudan University in 2019. This research journey also included founding a materials startup in 2022 and working at Tesla's Shanghai Gigafactory in 2023.
