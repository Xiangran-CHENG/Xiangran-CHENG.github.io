Join us

Build the materials we need, together.

We welcome incoming PhD students interested in connecting AI, synthesis science and experimental evidence.

How can we predict what will form, choose a viable synthesis path and verify that a material delivers its intended function? Join us in turning these questions into models, tools and experiments.

We are interested in people with backgrounds in materials science, chemistry, physics, computer science, robotics and related fields.

We value curiosity, courage, resilience and empathy. Our approach is to test ideas early, learn from evidence and pursue important questions over the long term.
