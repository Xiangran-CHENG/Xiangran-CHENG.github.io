I study how materials form, and how AI can help us make them.我研究材料如何形成，以及 AI 如何帮助我们合成材料。

Email邮箱 Google Scholar GitHub CV Portfolio & Gallery作品与图库

I am a Research Scientist at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, working at the intersection of materials science, AI and automation. My research connects reaction pathways and synthesizability with polymer electrolytes and battery interfaces.我是北京中关村学院研究科学家，研究材料科学、AI 与自动化的交叉问题。我关注反应路径与材料可合成性，以及聚合物电解质和电池界面。

A question that drives my work: can we predict how a synthesis reaction will unfold, and use that prediction to choose the next experiment? In the Cheng Research Group, we are exploring world models for materials synthesis and working toward autonomous laboratories that learn from experimental feedback.我最关心的问题是：能否预测合成反应如何演化，并据此选择下一步实验？在 Cheng Research Group，我们正在探索材料合成的世界模型，并致力于建设能够从实验反馈中学习的自主实验室。

Research研究

Reliable synthesis is our central question, supported by structure–function research, energy applications and scientific agents.以材料的可靠合成为核心，连接结构与功能研究、能源应用和科研智能体。

  1. 01

    Synthesizability and Synthesis Design可合成性与合成路径设计

    We study which precursors, pathways and conditions can reliably produce a target material. Metal nanoclusters, sulfide solid electrolytes and mesoporous materials provide research systems for connecting AI predictions with formation mechanisms and experimental feedback.研究哪些前驱体、反应路径和工艺条件能够可靠地形成目标材料。以金属纳米团簇、硫化物固态电解质和介孔材料为研究体系，将 AI 预测与形成机制及实验反馈结合起来。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  2. 02

    Structure Evolution and Functional Design结构演化与功能设计

    We develop models of material structures and their evolution, with research settings including ferroelectric domains and magnetic storage materials. Ferroelectric domain-structure prediction provides a starting point for exploring how evolving structures relate to functional response.发展材料结构及其演化的预测模型，研究场景包括铁电畴和磁性存储材料。以铁电畴结构预测为起点，进一步探索结构演化与功能响应之间的关系。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  3. 03

    Materials for Energy Storage and Conversion能源存储与转化材料

    Energy applications define material requirements and provide a setting for testing synthesis outcomes under working conditions. Current work focuses on electrolytes, cathodes and battery interfaces, with the aim of extending the approach to energy conversion.以能源应用提出材料需求，并在实际工作条件下检验合成材料的性能。目前聚焦电解质、正极与电池界面，并计划将这一研究方法拓展到能源转化。

    Ongoing projects →在研项目 →
  4. 04

    Scientific Agents and Autonomous Research科研智能体与自主研究

    We are developing agents that connect literature extraction, knowledge graphs, multimodal analysis and research tools. The goal is to use traceable evidence to propose hypotheses, select informative studies, analyze results and revise explanations with researcher oversight.发展连接文献提取、知识图谱、多模态分析与科研工具的智能体，目标是在研究者监督下，依据可追溯的证据提出假设、选择有信息价值的研究、分析结果并修正解释。
